Section 1: Leave Policy

ABC Corporation provides the following leave benefits to full-time employees:

1. Annual Paid Leave:
Employees are entitled to 20 working days of paid leave per calendar year. Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the next year.

2. Sick Leave:
Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave annually. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.

3. Maternity Leave:
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave as per government regulations.

4. Paternity Leave:
Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave.

5. Unpaid Leave:
Employees may apply for unpaid leave subject to manager approval.

---

Section 2: Work From Home Policy

ABC Corporation allows employees to work from home up to 2 days per week. Work from home requests must be approved by the reporting manager.

Employees working remotely must:
- Be available during core business hours (10 AM to 6 PM)
- Maintain data confidentiality
- Use company-provided laptops and VPN

Work from home is not allowed for roles requiring physical presence such as data center operations and security staff.

---

Section 3: Medical and Insurance Benefits

All full-time employees are covered under the company medical insurance plan.

Coverage includes:
- Hospitalization expenses up to INR 5,00,000 per year
- OPD consultation up to INR 10,000 per year
- Coverage for spouse and up to two dependent children

Insurance does not cover cosmetic surgery or experimental treatments.

---

Section 4: IT Security Policy

Employees must follow the IT security guidelines strictly:

- Passwords must be changed every 90 days
- Two-factor authentication is mandatory for VPN access
- USB storage devices are prohibited unless approved by IT security
- Confidential data must not be stored on personal devices
- Company email must be used for official communication

Violation of IT security policy may result in disciplinary action.

---

Section 5: Device Usage Policy

Employees are provided company laptops for official work only.

Rules:
- Installation of unauthorized software is prohibited
- Devices must be locked when unattended
- Antivirus software must be enabled at all times
- Devices should not be shared with non-employees

Loss of company devices must be reported within 24 hours to IT support.

---

Section 6: Performance and Appraisal Policy

Employees will undergo performance appraisal once every year in the month of March.

Performance evaluation criteria include:
- Technical skills
- Communication skills
- Project delivery
- Compliance with company policies

High-performing employees may receive:
- Salary increments
- Performance bonuses
- Promotions

---

Section 7: Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to:
- Maintain professional behavior
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Respect workplace diversity
- Not engage in harassment or discrimination
- Protect company reputation

Any violation of code of conduct will be investigated by HR and management.

---

Section 8: Travel Policy

Employees traveling for business purposes are entitled to reimbursement for:

- Economy class flight tickets
- Hotel stay up to INR 5,000 per night
- Daily food allowance up to INR 1,500
- Local transport expenses

Travel expenses must be submitted within 7 days of travel completion along with valid receipts.

---

Section 9: Data Privacy Policy

ABC Corporation is committed to protecting employee and customer data.

Key principles:
- Personal data must be collected only for business purposes
- Data access should be role-based
- Data must be encrypted at rest and in transit
- Data retention period is 5 years unless legally required otherwise

Employees must report any data breach within 2 hours to the IT security team.

---

Section 10: Training and Development

The company encourages continuous learning and development.

Employees are eligible for:
- 5 paid training days per year
- Access to online learning platforms
- Certification reimbursement up to INR 30,000 per year (subject to approval)
