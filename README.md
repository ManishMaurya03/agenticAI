2️⃣ Business Problem We Are Solving

Today, advisors:
	•	Spend 45–60 minutes preparing for each client meeting
	•	Manually review client goals, portfolio data, and market updates
	•	Face compliance risk in how insights are communicated
	•	Deliver inconsistent quality across advisors

This limits:
	•	Advisor productivity
	•	Personalization at scale
	•	Growth capacity

⸻

3️⃣ The Solution – Advisor Copilot

A secure, agentic AI system that:
	•	Analyzes client profile and goals
	•	Reviews portfolio performance and risk
	•	Filters relevant market developments
	•	Generates advisor-ready discussion points
	•	Applies built-in compliance guardrails

All in under 2 minutes.

⸻

4️⃣ Core Features

🔹 Personalized Client Insights

Understands life stage, goals, and risk tolerance to tailor every meeting brief.

🔹 Intelligent Portfolio Analysis

Highlights allocation, risk exposure, and performance trends — without autonomous recommendations.

🔹 Contextual Market Intelligence

Filters only market events relevant to the specific client’s holdings.

🔹 Compliance-Embedded Language

Ensures neutral, advisor-safe communication with built-in guardrails.

🔹 Secure, Private Architecture

Runs entirely inside the firm’s VPC. No client data leaves the organization.
