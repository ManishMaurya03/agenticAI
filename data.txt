Company Name: ABC Corporation
Document Type: Enterprise Policy and Operations Handbook

====================================================
Section 1: Leave Policy
====================================================

1. Annual Paid Leave:
Employees are entitled to 20 working days of paid leave per calendar year. Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the next year. Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal.

2. Sick Leave:
Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave annually. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.

3. Maternity Leave:
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave in accordance with government regulations.

4. Paternity Leave:
Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave.

5. Unpaid Leave:
Employees may apply for unpaid leave subject to manager and HR approval.

====================================================
Section 2: Work From Home Policy
====================================================

Employees are allowed to work from home up to 2 days per week with prior manager approval.

Remote employees must:
- Be available during core business hours from 10 AM to 6 PM
- Use company-approved VPN and devices
- Follow all data security guidelines
- Maintain professional conduct during virtual meetings

Work from home is not permitted for roles requiring physical presence such as facilities management and security staff.

====================================================
Section 3: Medical and Insurance Benefits
====================================================

All full-time employees are covered under the company medical insurance plan.

Coverage includes:
- Hospitalization expenses up to INR 5,00,000 per year
- OPD consultation expenses up to INR 10,000 per year
- Coverage for spouse and up to two dependent children

Exclusions:
- Cosmetic surgery
- Experimental treatments
- Non-prescribed medicines

====================================================
Section 4: IT Security Policy
====================================================

Employees must comply with IT security standards:

- Passwords must be changed every 90 days
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for VPN access
- USB storage devices are prohibited unless approved by IT security
- Confidential data must not be stored on personal devices
- Company email must be used for official communication

Security incidents must be reported within 1 hour to the IT security team.

Violation of IT security policies may result in disciplinary action.

====================================================
Section 5: Device Usage Policy
====================================================

Employees are issued company laptops for official use only.

Rules:
- Installation of unauthorized software is prohibited
- Devices must be locked when unattended
- Antivirus and firewall must be enabled at all times
- Devices should not be shared with non-employees

Loss or theft of company devices must be reported within 24 hours.

====================================================
Section 6: Performance and Appraisal Policy
====================================================

Performance appraisals are conducted annually in the month of March.

Evaluation criteria include:
- Technical skills
- Communication skills
- Project delivery
- Compliance with policies
- Team collaboration

High-performing employees may receive:
- Salary increments
- Performance bonuses
- Promotions

====================================================
Section 7: Code of Conduct
====================================================

Employees must:
- Maintain professional behavior
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Respect workplace diversity
- Not engage in harassment or discrimination
- Protect company reputation

Violations of the code of conduct will be investigated by HR and senior management.

====================================================
Section 8: Travel and Expense Policy
====================================================

Employees traveling for business purposes are eligible for reimbursement of:

- Economy class flight tickets
- Hotel accommodation up to INR 5,000 per night
- Daily meal allowance up to INR 1,500
- Local transportation expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 7 working days along with valid receipts.

====================================================
Section 9: Data Privacy and Retention Policy
====================================================

ABC Corporation is committed to protecting employee and customer data.

Key principles:
- Personal data must be collected only for business purposes
- Access to data must be role-based
- Data must be encrypted at rest and in transit
- Data retention period is 5 years unless legally required otherwise

Any data breach must be reported within 2 hours to the Data Protection Officer.

====================================================
Section 10: Training and Certification Policy
====================================================

Employees are eligible for:
- 5 paid training days per year
- Access to internal and external learning platforms
- Certification reimbursement up to INR 30,000 per year subject to manager approval

Employees must submit certificates for reimbursement within 30 days of course completion.

====================================================
Section 11: Incident Management Policy
====================================================

An incident is defined as any unplanned interruption to a business service.

Incident categories:
- Critical: System outage affecting all users
- High: Major functionality unavailable
- Medium: Partial service degradation
- Low: Minor issues

Response timelines:
- Critical: 15 minutes
- High: 30 minutes
- Medium: 2 hours
- Low: 1 business day

====================================================
Section 12: Change Management Policy
====================================================

All system changes must follow the change management process.

Change types:
- Standard Change: Pre-approved low-risk changes
- Normal Change: Requires CAB approval
- Emergency Change: For critical incidents

All changes must be documented and tested before production deployment.

====================================================
Section 13: Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery
====================================================

The company maintains a disaster recovery plan to ensure business continuity.

Objectives:
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 4 hours
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 1 hour

Backups are taken daily and stored in a secure offsite location.

Disaster recovery drills are conducted twice a year.

====================================================
Section 14: Procurement and Vendor Management
====================================================

All purchases must follow the procurement policy.

Rules:
- Three vendor quotations are required for purchases above INR 50,000
- Contracts must be approved by the legal department
- Vendors must comply with security and data privacy requirements

Payments are processed within 30 days of invoice submission.

====================================================
Section 15: Financial Policy
====================================================

Employees must follow financial governance rules:

- All expenses must be business-related
- Fraud or misuse of company funds will result in termination
- Financial records must be maintained for audit purposes

Annual audits are conducted by external auditors.

====================================================
Section 16: Customer Support Policy
====================================================

Customer support operates from 9 AM to 9 PM on business days.

Service Level Agreements (SLA):
- Critical issues: Resolution within 4 hours
- High priority issues: Resolution within 8 hours
- Medium priority issues: Resolution within 24 hours
- Low priority issues: Resolution within 3 business days

====================================================
Section 17: Cloud and Infrastructure Policy
====================================================

All cloud resources must be provisioned through the IT department.

Rules:
- Production systems must use approved cloud providers
- Encryption must be enabled for storage and network traffic
- Access must follow least privilege principle
- Logs must be retained for 90 days

====================================================
Section 18: AI and Data Usage Policy
====================================================

AI systems must be used responsibly.

Guidelines:
- Do not input confidential data into external AI tools
- AI outputs must be reviewed by humans
- Bias and fairness must be evaluated regularly
- AI models must be monitored for drift

====================================================
Section 19: Remote Access Policy
====================================================

Remote access is permitted only via secure VPN.

Rules:
- VPN sessions expire after 8 hours
- MFA is mandatory
- Access logs are audited weekly

Unauthorized remote access is strictly prohibited.

====================================================
Section 20: Exit and Asset Recovery Policy
====================================================

Upon resignation or termination:

- Employees must return company devices on the last working day
- System access will be revoked immediately
- Final settlement will be processed within 30 days
- Exit interviews are mandatory
